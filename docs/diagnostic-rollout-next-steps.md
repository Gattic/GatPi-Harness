## Next evidence cohorts

### Learning and skill synthesis

The original reviewed C2/C3/C5 protocol remains historical and is not silently relabeled. The current first automation layer is a new preregistered machine-verification cohort: freeze executable repository checks before exposure, automatically capture their proof, retain missing outcomes, and compare independent control/candidate sessions at a fixed horizon. A statistical and cost pass can automatically enable only the exact advisory reminder. No human outcome reviews or model self-ratings are required. This delivery-completeness endpoint is not general skill-quality evidence or authorization for synthesis.

### Code-context retrieval

Keep advisory mode and preserve the original 30-task candidate packet without fabricating approvals. Autonomous promotion needs a separately frozen executable source/test oracle and fresh provider provenance; command success or commit-touched paths alone do not establish semantic relevance. A new machine-grounded protocol must not reuse the human-gold cohort as if it had already satisfied that requirement. `select-tests` remains additive to baseline tests; default-on requires separate noninferiority evidence.

### Extension portfolio

Keep opportunity telemetry and historical judgments separate. Invocation counts, successful tool returns, and explicit opinions are not causal usefulness evidence. An automatic lifecycle decision needs an executable task endpoint, preregistered comparison, exact source identity, session-qualified denominators and current-session exclusion. Until those exist, the machine decision is no change—not a request for a human approval label.

### Entropy, timing, and trace diagnostics

Keep monitoring advisory and payload capture off. Logging overhead is directly measurable; warning actionability is not implied by a score or a failed command. Automated branch/reset/consolidation requires a separately preregistered reversible intervention with independent incident criteria and counterfactual benefit measurement. Preserve the original incident records and missing outcomes. Missing causal evidence blocks interventions without demanding human labels. Use retention receipts to confirm storage remains bounded; do not silently delete protected historical traces.
