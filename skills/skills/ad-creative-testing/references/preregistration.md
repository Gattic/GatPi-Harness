# Creative experiment pre-registration (template)

**Objective:** user business outcome and primary KPI.

**Eligibility:** traffic platform, audience, geography, device, consent, known exclusion criteria.

**Hypothesis:** a **specific** mechanism (e.g., showing product use rather than a static packshot changes qualified purchase intent) that can be falsified.

**Control:** stable creative and destination. **Treatment:** exact changed feature and files, with all other conditions held fixed when practical.

**Assignment:** user/session/ad impression unit; plan for cross-device overlaps, campaigns, bidding algorithms and user reexposure. Creative auction experiments are not automatically perfect A/B tests.

**Primary metric:** numerator, denominator, attribution window and deduplication. **Guardrails:** refunds, complaints, misleading clicks, page load, unsubscribes and profitability.

**Power assumptions:** baseline, minimum effect of practical interest, traffic and multiple testing correction. Avoid guessed certainties: state each estimate and calculate before the test.

**Stopping:** predeclared duration or information threshold, adverse-risk threshold and handling of outages.

**Analysis:** estimate with interval and sample sizes, missingness, crossover, preplanned slices versus exploratory slices, incremental cost per accepted result.

**Release gate:** legal/platform claim review, inclusive targeting and accessible creative. No manipulation through artificial pressure or fake evidence.
