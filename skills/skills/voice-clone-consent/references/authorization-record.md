# Authorized voice synthesis record

**Speaker authorization:** signed/recorded affirmative permission by the identifiable voice owner or an authorized representative, with means to verify scope. Public availability of samples is not a substitute.

Record **speaker**, **purpose**, **output media**, **target audience**, **distribution venues**, **languages**, **whether disclosure appears**, **model/provider**, **reference audio IDs**, **storage duration**, **data processors**, **revocation method**, **identity disclosure**, and **reviewer/consent date**. Do not store copies of identity documents in a skill folder or output log.

Keep an immutable provenance reference, but allow legitimate deletion/withdrawal of source audio where contracts and law permit. Do not retain more personal data than the stated purpose requires. Review output meaning and possible misleading endorsement separately from acoustic quality. For sensitive or public-facing uses, provide clear synthetic-audio disclosure.
